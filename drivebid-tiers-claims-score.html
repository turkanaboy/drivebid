What counts as a "deal closing"?
When you mark a deal as accepted in DriveBid, or after 90 days of inactivity on an unlocked request, the unlock period ends. Your free account stays active forever — only the $20 unlock resets.
Do I need to unlock to get good offers?
No. Many buyers get great offers on the free tier — especially in metro areas with lots of nearby dealers. The unlock makes most sense if you're in a smaller market, looking for a rare vehicle, or want to run multiple searches at once.
Why $20 and not a monthly subscription?
Because you buy a car once every few years, not every month. A subscription would make no sense for most buyers. We'd rather charge a fair one-time fee that's clearly tied to the value of the deal you're working on.

How claims work

The 24-hour claim window

When a dealer claims your request, you get 24 hours of exclusive priority to respond — accept, negotiate, or pass. This protects dealers from investing time in offers that go nowhere, and keeps the marketplace moving.

01
Dealer claims your request
A verified dealer reviews your request and decides to submit an offer. Your 24-hour window starts immediately.
02
You get notified instantly
Email and in-app notification with the offer details and a clear countdown. One extension of 24hrs is available if you need more time.
03
Accept, negotiate, or pass
Accepting opens the full messaging thread. Negotiating sends a counter. Passing releases the slot — no penalty to your score.
04
Next dealer can claim
If you pass or the window expires without response, the next matched dealer can claim. Unanswered windows affect your responsiveness score.

Buyer accountability

Your responsiveness score

Your score reflects how reliably you respond to dealer claims. It's visible to dealers before they invest time in your request — and rewards serious buyers with more attention.

✅
What counts in your favor
Responding within the 24-hour window (accept, negotiate, or deny) always counts positively — even if you pass on the offer. Active engagement is what matters.
⏰
What hurts your score
Letting the clock expire without any response is the only thing that negatively affects your score. Denying offers does not count against you — only silence does.
🔒
Clock pauses fairly
If a dealer goes quiet mid-negotiation, your countdown pauses automatically. You won't be penalized for the dealer's slow response.
📈
Score recovers over time
A missed window has less impact as you build a longer history of positive responses. New users start with a neutral score — no penalty for being new.
